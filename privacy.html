Juswokup Privacy Policy
Juswokup · Etsy shop and Pin Publisher app

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Juswokup Pin Publisher is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the Juswokup Etsy shop, to post pins about the shop's own products to the owner's own Pinterest boards. It does not collect, store or share information about anyone else.

Who we are

Juswokup is a small Australian shop selling digital printable products on Etsy at juswokup.etsy.com. "Juswokup Pin Publisher" is the name of the Pinterest app the shop owner uses to publish pins.

What the app accesses on Pinterest

With the owner's permission, given through Pinterest's own sign-in screen, the app can:

  • read the names and IDs of the owner's boards, so the owner can choose where a pin goes (boards:read);
  • read the owner's own pins, to confirm a pin was created (pins:read);
  • create pins on the owner's boards, each showing a Juswokup product image with a title, description and a link to the product on Etsy (pins:write).

The app does not access other people's accounts, messages, followers, ads, billing or payment information.

How the sign-in is kept safe

  • The access token Pinterest issues is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file outside the shop's project files, readable only by the owner.
  • The token is never shared, sold, published or sent anywhere except back to Pinterest to make the requests listed above.
  • Every pin is checked and approved by the owner before it is posted.
  • The owner can remove the app's access at any time in Pinterest settings, under apps that can access your account.

Shoppers and visitors

The app collects no information about people who see or click the pins. Clicking a pin opens the product on Etsy, where Etsy's own privacy policy applies. Purchases, payments and downloads are handled entirely by Etsy. Juswokup receives only the order details Etsy provides to sellers, and uses them only to support that order.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The app uses no cookies, tracking or advertising. Pinterest and Etsy may show the owner standard statistics about their own pins and listings within their own platforms.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For any question about privacy, send a message to the Juswokup shop through Etsy at juswokup.etsy.com.
